# V2.2 experimental-model production gate

The new Elo, Poisson/Dixon-Coles-style, lineup-continuity and context residuals do not automatically change the primary fair probability.

A future production correction should require, at minimum:

- chronological out-of-sample improvement over the market baseline;
- adequate sample size;
- acceptable calibration/Brier performance;
- positive live closing-line evidence after the residual was first observed;
- no material performance collapse when a candidate feature is removed/retested;
- strict proof that every input existed before the relevant kickoff.

Until then these components remain visible research evidence only. This prevents the application from turning additional public football information into unvalidated confidence.
